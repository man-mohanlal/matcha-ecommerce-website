The Valley Chronicle

From The Sacred Valleys To Your Daily Chawan

Failda Matcha was forged out of a quiet rebellion against mass commercial blending. In recent decades, industrial demand transformed matcha into an undifferentiated green commodity—combining varied grades, multiple origins, and heat-burned factory pulverization.

Together with Master Chashi Kenjiro Takahashi, we anchored our practice exclusively to smallholder generational plots in Wazuka and Uji. Here, soil chemistry is elevated through decomposed fallen cryptomeria needles and river mineral silt, resulting in first-flush Tencha leaves rich in raw L-theanine and delicate umami without the astringency of industrialized crops.

“Real matcha is not merely tea; it is a quiet discipline of time, morning fog, and living stone.”

Mindful Preparation

The Daily Architecture Of Calm

Matcha preparation is not a chore—it is an intentional sensory pause. Sifting, measuring, whisking in the rhythmic “W” pattern awakens both the natural L-theanine and a state of tranquil alertness (*zanshin*).

01 Sift 2g (approx. 2 chashaku scoops) ceremonial tencha into chawan.
02 Pour 70ml soft water cooled to precisely 75°C - 80°C (167°F - 176°F).
03 Whisk briskly from wrist for 20 seconds until a velvety, fine foam rises.
